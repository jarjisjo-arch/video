# Gemfinity privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Gemfinity is a match-3 puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

## What Gemfinity collects

Nothing. Gemfinity doesn't ask you to create an account, doesn't show ads, doesn't use analytics or tracking tools, and doesn't collect, sell or share personal information.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, scores, boosters, lives, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling Gemfinity deletes it.

## Internet use

When the Android app starts, it checks for game updates by downloading a small file from GitHub, where Gemfinity's files are hosted. When an update is available, it also downloads the new game content (levels, pictures and sounds) from GitHub. These requests carry no personal information about you. Like any website, GitHub receives standard connection details such as your IP address; GitHub handles them under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). If you installed Gemfinity from Google Play, updates to the app itself come through Google Play under Google's privacy policy.

## Permissions

Gemfinity uses one Android permission: internet access, for the update check described above. The game plays offline.

## Children

Gemfinity is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
