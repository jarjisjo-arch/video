## Full description (1318/4000 characters)

```
Dig into a glowing crystal mine with Digby the mole miner! Swap neighbouring gems to line up three or more, set off dazzling special gems, and work your way down through 81 handcrafted levels across 9 painted worlds. Then keep going in the Endless Dig, where a fresh level waits every time.

MATCH, BLAST, DIG
• Swap gems to match 3 or more of the same kind
• Match 4 for a Streak gem that clears a whole row or column
• Match in an L or T shape for a Burst gem that explodes twice
• Line up 5 for a Prism that clears every gem of one colour
• Swap two special gems together for giant combos

9 WORLDS TO EXPLORE
Amber Caverns, Crystal Mine, Glowing Depths, Frozen Grotto, Lava Forge, Sunken Ruins, Heart of the Mountain, Golden Vault and Overgrown Temple, each with its own map and music.

NEW CHALLENGES IN EVERY WORLD
Crack glowing ice, break stone blocks, snap chains, stop creeping crystal before it spreads, and match fuse bombs before they go off. Bring treasure chests down to the bottom to collect them.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Three board styles: gems, fruit or planets
• Hammer and Shuffle boosters, plus free hints
• Daily treasure with a 7-day streak
• Gem Rush: spare moves turn into bonus blasts at the end of a level
• New levels arrive as free updates
• Plays offline. No ads, no purchases, no account needed
```
