# Moonpull privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Moonpull does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The game works fully offline. It makes no network requests.
- There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
- Your progress (stars, fireflies, settings) is saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the game or choosing Settings > Start over erases it.
- When you use Share or Photo mode, the game hands a picture to your phone's own share sheet. What happens next is up to the app you choose.
- The game asks for one permission: vibration, for touch feedback. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Moonpull is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
